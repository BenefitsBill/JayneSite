Services and Fees

Initial Consultation

Every client's journey starts with a 15-minute no-obligation consultation, free of charge. This session allows us to assess whether we are suited to work together, and it gives you the opportunity to ask any questions you may have before starting your therapy journey.

Payment and Cancellations

Fee of a Counselling Session
A 50 minute counselling session is £65.00. Payment by Bank transfer before the session. Bank details will be given on request
Cancellation
To cancel a counselling session a minimum of 24 hours’ notice prior to the session has to be given by the client, otherwise the full fee for the session has to be paid.
